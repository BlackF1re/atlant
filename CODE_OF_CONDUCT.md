# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We want AtlANTian to be a practical, technically rigorous and welcoming project.
Treat other participants with respect. Discuss ideas, evidence and trade-offs
without personal attacks, harassment, discrimination or unwanted contact.

## Expected behaviour

- Give reproducible evidence when challenging a technical claim.
- Assume good faith and keep feedback focused on the work.
- Respect safety boundaries around mains-powered equipment, FPGA pin routing and
  destructive storage operations.
- Do not publish private contact details, credentials or unpatched security
  vulnerabilities.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, hide or remove content that breaches this code, and
may restrict participation for repeated or serious violations.

For a private conduct concern, contact the maintainer through the contact link on
the [BlackF1re GitHub profile](https://github.com/BlackF1re). Security issues must
follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead of being reported publicly.
