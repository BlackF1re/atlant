# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We want AtlANTian to be a practical, technically rigorous and welcoming
project. Treat other participants with respect. Discuss ideas, evidence and
trade-offs without personal attacks, harassment, discrimination or unwanted
contact.

## Expected behaviour

- Give reproducible evidence when challenging a technical claim.
- Assume good faith and keep feedback focused on the work.
- Respect safety boundaries around mains-powered equipment, FPGA pin routing
  and destructive storage operations.
- Do not publish private contact details, credentials or unpatched security
  vulnerabilities.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, hide or remove content that breaches this code,
and may restrict participation for repeated or serious violations. To report a
concern privately, use the repository owner's GitHub contact channel. Security
issues must follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
